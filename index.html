The route

How your tiffin gets to you

Borrowed from a hundred-year-old idea: Mumbai's dabbawalas route thousands of home-cooked lunches a day using nothing but a coded lid. We've rebuilt that same reliability for delivery.

01

Pick your trail

Choose your meals for the week from a rotating menu of regional classics — vegetarian and non-vegetarian, clearly marked.

02

A home cook makes it fresh

Prepared to order the same morning it's delivered, in a certified home kitchen near you — not a central factory.

03

It arrives in a steel tiffin

Hot, sealed, and packed tier by tier, the way your own family would pack it.

04

Return the box, repeat

We pick up the empty tiffin on your next delivery, wash it, and send it back out. Nothing goes to landfill.

Why we started

Home food, for people far from home

Tiffin Trails started with a simple problem: the food that actually feels like home rarely comes from a restaurant kitchen. It comes from someone's mother, aunt, or neighbour — cooked in small batches, with no shortcuts.

We built Tiffin Trails to bring that food to more doors, without turning it into factory food along the way. Every kitchen on our trail is still a home kitchen. Every cook still decides the recipe.
